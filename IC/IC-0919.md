# In-Class Exercise 0919 — Mochi's Cat Café: Statistics with AI

統計學（專班）· Chapters 1–3 · File: `mochi-cafe-30days.csv` · Total: about 30 minutes

**No coding needed.** Use any AI you like: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini… You ask in simple words (中文 or English). The AI makes the tables and charts. **You think like a statistician:** Is the answer right? What does it mean?

## The story

Ms. Lin opened a small café with her cat, **Mochi**. She wrote down 30 days of data. She asks: *"Should I hire help on weekends? Should I make more iced drinks on hot days?"*

| Column | Meaning | Column | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `day` | day 1 to 30 | `wait_minutes` | how long customers waited |
| `weekday` | Mon to Sun | `top_drink` | best-selling drink |
| `weather` | Sunny / Cloudy / Rainy | `mochi_naps` | how many naps Mochi took |
| `cups_sold` | cups sold that day | `temperature_c` | highest temperature (°C) |
| | | `iced_drinks` | iced drinks sold that day |

Ms. Lin's diary: **Day 1** — "The coffee machine broke." **Day 20** — "A famous influencer came. Very busy!"

## Session 1: Graphs (Ch. 1) · 5 min

**Q1.**

> 請畫 cups_sold 和 wait_minutes 的直方圖。 *Draw histograms of cups_sold and wait_minutes.*

(a) What is the shape of each? (mound-shaped / skewed right / skewed left) **[2 min]**
(b) Which days look unusual (outliers)? Use the diary. Should Ms. Lin delete them? **[3 min]**

## Session 2: Numbers (Ch. 2) · 10 min

**Q2.**

> 請算 wait_minutes 的平均數和中位數。再算 cups_sold 的標準差：有第20天和沒有第20天。 *Find the mean and median of wait_minutes. Then find the standard deviation of cups_sold, with and without day 20.*

(a) Which is bigger, the mean or the median? Why? (Look at your Q1 picture.) **[2 min]**
(b) Without day 20, does the standard deviation get bigger or smaller? By how much? **[2 min]**
(c) Ask your AI: *"Did you divide by n or n − 1?"* Our textbook uses n − 1. **[1 min]**

**Q3.**

> 請算第20天 cups_sold 的 z 分數。用課本方法 (Q1 在 0.25(n+1) 的位置) 算 Q1、Q3、IQR 和上界，並畫盒形圖。 *Find the z-score of day 20 for cups_sold. Use the textbook rule (Q1 at position 0.25(n + 1)) to find Q1, Q3, IQR and the upper fence. Draw a box plot.*

(a) Write the z-score and the upper fence. **[3 min]**
(b) Is day 20 an outlier by both rules? **[2 min]**

## Session 3: Two variables (Ch. 3) · 10 min

**Q4. Hire weekend help?**

> 請比較週末 (Sat, Sun) 和平日的 cups_sold 中位數，並畫並排盒形圖。 *Compare the median cups_sold on weekends (Sat, Sun) and weekdays. Draw side-by-side box plots.*

(a) Write the two medians. Should Ms. Lin hire help on weekends? One sentence. **[3 min]**

**Q5. Iced drinks on hot days?**

> 請畫 temperature_c (x) 和 iced_drinks (y) 的散佈圖。算 r 和迴歸線，並預測 33°C 的冰飲杯數。 *Draw a scatterplot of temperature_c (x) and iced_drinks (y). Find r and the regression line. Predict iced drinks at 33 °C.*

(a) Write r, the line, and your prediction. **[2 min]**
(b) Say the slope in simple words: "1 °C hotter → about ___ more iced drinks." **[1 min]**
(c) The hottest day in the data is 32.7 °C. Can we trust a prediction for 38 °C? **[1 min]**

**Q6. A trap!**

> 請算 cups_sold 和 wait_minutes 的 r。再去掉第1天和第20天，算一次。 *Find r between cups_sold and wait_minutes. Then remove days 1 and 20 and find r again.*

(a) What happened to r? Does "busy days cause long waits" follow from this data? **[3 min]**
