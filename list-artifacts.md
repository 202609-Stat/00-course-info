## Ch. 7 Sampling Distributions

- ⏳ Demonstrate the Central Limit Theorem
  - Sample size = n
  - Replications = R
  - Population: Bernoulli, Uniform(0, 1), Normal, Poisson(5), Exp(10)
  - In the panels: show one sample of size n (X₁, X₂, …, Xₙ) at a time, its sample mean, and its normalized sample mean.
  - Show the histograms of X₁ (overlaid with the true PDF), the sample mean, and the normalized sample mean (overlaid with the true PDF).
