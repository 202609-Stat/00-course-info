Chi-Square from Z²

Chi-square with 1 df: square a standard normal

This demo shows how the chi-square distribution with 1 degree of freedom is built from \(Z^2\).
① Density of \(Z \sim N(0,1)\): \[f_Z(z)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\,e^{-z^2/2},\qquad -\infty \lt z \lt \infty.\]② Density of \(Y=Z^2 \sim \chi^2(1)\): \[f_Y(y)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi y}}\,e^{-y/2},\qquad y \gt 0.\]Below, each new dot in panel 1 is one value of \(Z\), and each new dot in panel 2 is one value of \(Z^2\). Watch the dots stack up from 0, one draw at a time, until they take the shape of the two densities above. Panels 3 and 4 summarize the distribution of the simulated data with histograms, overlaid with the theoretical density (black line) for comparison.
